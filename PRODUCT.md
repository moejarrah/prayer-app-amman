# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This is a personal prayer-times app for the creator, with enough polish and reliability to share with family. Users are likely checking it quickly on a phone, often from the home screen, and need the current prayer, next prayer, countdown, and monthly reference without friction.

## Product Purpose

The app provides Jordan prayer times from the Ministry of Awqaf, works after the first load, and stays simple enough to maintain as the data and features evolve.

## Brand Personality

Calm, respectful, practical. The interface should feel quiet and dependable rather than commercial or decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid overbuilt dashboards, marketing-style landing pages, excessive animation, fragile custom UI, and designs that make the prayer time harder to read at a glance.

## Design Principles

- Make the current prayer and next countdown obvious in one glance.
- Keep phone ergonomics first, especially installed-app safe areas.
- Prefer boring reliability over cleverness.
- Keep the code small enough that future changes are easy.
- Preserve offline use as a core feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support touch targets of at least 44px, readable contrast across day/night themes, reduced-motion preferences, semantic controls where practical, and Arabic RTL layout as the default.
